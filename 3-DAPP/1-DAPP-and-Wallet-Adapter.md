# DAAP (Decentralized Application):

DAAP can **connect** to your Wallet and ask for **permissions** to your wallet to make **transactions** and other activities. Any website that lets you connect your wallet to the website, lets you do something with your funds, is called a **DAPP**.

**Example:** https://anza-xyz.github.io/wallet-adapter/example/ 

# Wallet Adapter:

**Wallet Adapter** is the **library** that **provides** all the **functions** to make a **DAPP**. It works with various wallets. A wallet adapter lets a **DAPP** **connect** to a **wallet**. There are many different types of Wallet Adapters that you can use.

Now, Lets make a DAAP on our own using the **Solana Wallet Adapter (Anza-XYZ Wallet Adapter)**.
